# Rigid block on a consistent surface foundation

An original unit hex is coupled kinematically to its central reference point.
Rotations are held; the three translations are free. A=1 m^2, mass=2 kg,
k_n=100 N/m^3 along x and k_t=200 N/m^3 in y/z. F=(1,2,3) N.
The coefficients are multiplied by 1, 1e-4 and 1e6, keeping geometry and
mass fixed. This checks three frequencies, shear compliance and the
rigid-displacement limits u proportional to 1/k and f proportional to sqrt(k).
The very large low-k displacement is an algebraic test of a linear model,
not a claim of physically valid small deformation.

The zero-k static singularity was also observed and preserved in the lane
verification record; this success-only oracle does not execute that expected
error. The limit test concerns a rigid body, not convergence of deformable
solid stresses to a fully fixed-boundary solution.
