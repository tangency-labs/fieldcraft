# Closed pipes under internal pressure (Lamé)

Two straight pipes of two beam2 elements each, clamped at one end and closed, under internal pressure. The thick
one (a = 10, b = 20, p = 100) is checked against Lamé's wall stresses, the closed-end axial stress and the stretch.
The thin one (r_m = 100, t = 5, p = 5) is checked against Lamé's bore hoop stress, 100.0625, which the membrane
formula p r_m/t = 100 approximates to (t/2r_m)² = 6.25 × 10⁻⁴.
