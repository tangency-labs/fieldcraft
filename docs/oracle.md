## Sources and tolerances

- **[arc-shallow-arch](../tests/oracle/arc-shallow-arch/)** (theory): Snap-through of a shallow arch by arc length. Source: One-mode closed form of a shallow sinusoidal arch y₀ = a sin(πx/L) on pins at a fixed span under q₀ sin(πx/L): q₀ = EI k⁴ [c + (a − c)(2ac − c²)/(4r²)], k = π/L, limit points at c = a ∓ r √((a²/r² − 4)/3) (S. P. Timoshenko, J. M. Gere, Theory of Elastic Stability, 2nd ed., McGraw-Hill 1961, §7.6; Y. C. Fung, A. Kaplan, NACA TN 2840, 1952): L = 100, h = 1, unit width, E = 10⁴, ν = 0, a/r = 3.5; the load is scaled to the upper limit load, so the peak load factor is 1 and the valley 0.211035. Tolerances — peak: 40 × 2 × 1 hex20 against shallow Euler–Bernoulli arch theory, the load piecewise constant per element, and the limit load picked from converged states (second order in the arc); valley: as the peak; the valley is a fifth of the peak load and a small difference of the bending and axial terms, so its tolerance is stated as 2 % of the peak load; negative: the inertia of the LDLᵀ factor: exactly one unstable direction between the limit points
- **[bar-pressure-wave](../tests/oracle/bar-pressure-wave/)** (theory): Elastic wave in a bar from a pressure step (explicit). Source: d'Alembert's solution of the one-dimensional wave equation: c = √(E/ρ), particle velocity v = p/(ρc); Graff, Wave Motion in Elastic Solids, Dover 1991, §2.1. Tolerances — u_end: the end moves at the particle velocity p/(ρc) from the start; the discrete wave's ringing behind the front changes the end's displacement by well under 1%; v_mid: central differences ring behind a step front (dispersion); bulk viscosity damps it within a few elements, leaving about 1% twenty elements behind; balance: external work = kinetic + internal + dissipated energy; the solver warns above 1%
- **[bar-rigid-wall-impact](../tests/oracle/bar-rigid-wall-impact/)** (theory): Elastic bar striking a rigid wall (explicit). Source: One-dimensional elastic impact on a rigid wall: contact time 2L/c, rebound at the impact speed, contact stress ρcv₀; Graff, Wave Motion in Elastic Solids, Dover 1991, §2.3; Johnson, Impact Strength of Materials, Arnold 1972, §1.6. Tolerances — u_front: the contact ends at 2L/c in the elastic theory; the discrete release front is spread over a few elements, a few tenths of a percent of the time, and the rebound speed is v₀ to about 1%; u_back: as for the struck end; ke: an elastic impact keeps the kinetic energy; quadratic bulk viscosity in the compression front and the vibration left in the bar take about 1%
- **[buckle-euler-cantilever](../tests/oracle/buckle-euler-cantilever/)** (theory): Euler buckling of a clamped–free column of solids. Source: Euler's column with Engesser's shear correction P = P_e/(1 + P_e/(κ G A)), κ = 5/6 (S. P. Timoshenko, J. M. Gere, Theory of Elastic Stability, 2nd ed., McGraw-Hill 1961, §2.1, §2.17): square section 1 × 1, E = 1000, ν = 0, length 10 clamped–free, effective length 20, P_e = π² E I/20², so σ_cr = P/A = 2.046071. Tolerances — lambda: hex20, 2 × 2 across and 10 along: the 3D solid's bending and shear against beam theory, and the preload concentrated at the held face
- **[buckle-euler-pinned](../tests/oracle/buckle-euler-pinned/)** (theory): Euler buckling of a pinned column of solids. Source: Euler's column with Engesser's shear correction P = P_e/(1 + P_e/(κ G A)), κ = 5/6 (S. P. Timoshenko, J. M. Gere, Theory of Elastic Stability, 2nd ed., McGraw-Hill 1961, §2.1, §2.17): square section 1 × 1, E = 1000, ν = 0, effective length 20, P_e = π² E I/20², so σ_cr = P/A = 2.046071. Tolerances — lambda: hex20, 2 × 2 across and 10 along: the 3D solid's bending and shear against beam theory, and the preload concentrated at the held face
- **[buckle-plate-square](../tests/oracle/buckle-plate-square/)** (theory): Buckling of a simply supported square plate in uniaxial compression. Source: Bryan's plate buckling stress σ_cr = k π² E/(12 (1 − ν²)) (t/b)² with k = 4 for a square plate simply supported on four edges (G. H. Bryan, Proc. London Math. Soc. 22:54–67, 1891; Timoshenko & Gere 1961, §9.2): b = 50, t = 0.5, E = 1000, ν = 0.3, σ_cr = 0.361524. Tolerances — lambda: a quarter plate of 10 × 10 × 2 hex20 (b/t = 100) supported on its bottom edge lines: the solid against thin-plate theory (−1.5 % at b/t = 50, falling with t/b) and the discretisation
